✦ The Artisan Journey

Art Created as a Form of Divine Prayer

Welcome to Evara Art. The word “Evara” is an ancient derivation signifying Gift of God. To lead artist Shraddha Parmar, painting is never an assembly line or an automated process. It is a quiet meditation, a form of spiritual communion where sacred imagination is transformed into tangible, textured wall art for your home sanctuary.

“Welcome to Evara Art – where creativity comes to life through every brushstroke. ‘Evara’ means Gift of God and I believe the art I create is truly a blessing and a form of divine expression. Each piece is crafted with passion, turning imagination into meaningful and beautiful artwork that honors divine patience.”
— Shraddha Parmar, Founder & Lead Artist

In a modern world saturated with flat, digital inkjet prints and synthetic mass factory decor, Evara Art was founded with a singular, uncompromising purpose: to bring back the soulful presence of authentic human touch. When you touch an Evara canvas or Lippan panel, you feel the raised ridges of the heavy impasto pigment, the coolness of hand-set glass mirrors, and the palpable tranquility imbued by days of patient devotion.

Heritage Discipline I

Sacred Devotional Canvas Paintings

Dedicated depictions of Lord Krishna, Radha Rani, Mahadev Shiva, and sacred Pichwai lotus motifs. Each composition begins on seasoned kiln-dried wooden frames, hand-stretched with heavyweight canvas, layered with luminous European acrylic pigments, and sealed with UV-resistant museum varnish.

  • 100% Original hand-painted brushwork • Never digital prints
  • Warm, soothing palettes designed for living rooms & prayer alcoves
  • Luminous details that radiate warmth in both daylight and ambient warm lighting
Heritage Discipline II

Traditional Gujarat Lippan & Clay Mirror Craft

Born from the rural artisan hamlets of Kutch, Gujarat, Lippan Kaam is an ancient mud-relief architecture craft. We elevate this sacred tradition into contemporary luxury art pieces using durable fortified clay dough, hand-rolled relief borders, and hundreds of individually placed mirror facets.

  • Authentic clay dough formulated for zero cracking and decades of strength
  • Convex and geometric hand-trimmed mirror facets that dance with ambient light
  • Sacred geometric mandalas, auspicious Ganesha, and Gayatri motifs

Pan-India Shipping & Delivery Policy

Every original painting and Lippan artwork is hand-crated with care to guarantee pristine, damage-free arrival at your doorstep.

1. Insured Delivery & Shipping Charges

We provide fully insured express doorstep delivery across all serviceable postal PIN codes in India through top-tier courier networks including Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC, and Xpressbees.

✦ Free Shipping Threshold: All domestic orders with a cart subtotal of ₹2,999 and above qualify for 100% Free Pan-India Delivery. For orders below ₹2,999, a flat insured shipping fee of ₹150 is applied at checkout.

2. Studio Processing & Dispatch Timelines

Because Evara Art creates genuine handcrafted originals rather than automated mass prints, every artwork undergoes careful final curing, moisture-seal varnishing, and structural inspection before dispatch:

Artwork Category Studio Handcraft & Preparation Transit Time (Post-Dispatch)
Ready-to-Ship Canvas Paintings 2 – 4 Business Days (Varnish & Quality Check) 3 – 6 Business Days
Handcrafted Lippan Mirror Art 3 – 5 Business Days (Clay Curing & Mirror Inspection) 4 – 7 Business Days
Bespoke Custom Commissions 7 – 14 Business Days (Collaborative Painting & Drying) 3 – 6 Business Days

*Business days exclude Sundays and Indian National Holidays. Severe weather conditions or regional transport restrictions may occasionally extend courier transit windows.

3. Armored Wooden Crating Standard

We understand that delicate canvas duck fabric and hand-cut Lippan mirrors require exceptional structural reinforcement. Every package is packed using our 4-tier packaging protocol:

  • Layer 1 – Anti-Moisture Barrier: Archival glassine wrap and dust-proof protective plastic sheathing.
  • Layer 2 – Impact Air-Cushion: Triple-layer 10mm shock-absorbent air bubble wrap.
  • Layer 3 – Reinforced Corner Guards: High-density rigid foam caps on all 4 corners to shield against drops.
  • Layer 4 – Rigid Wooden Hardboard Outer Crating: Solid composite hardboard sandwiching the artwork with tamper-evident security strapping.

4. Live Tracking & Delivery Updates

The moment your artwork is handed over to our insured logistics partner, you will receive an automatic dispatch notification with a direct tracking link via WhatsApp and SMS. You can follow your parcel’s live movement directly to your doorstep.

Returns, Replacement & Refund Policy

We take sacred pride in every brushstroke. In the rare event of transit damage or dissatisfaction, here is how we protect you.

1. 100% Zero-Risk Transit Damage Guarantee

Original art is fragile. Despite our multi-layered wooden crating, if your artwork sustains any damage during transit, Evara Art takes 100% financial and replacement responsibility. You will never be left at a loss.

✦ 48-Hour Unboxing Notice Protocol:
Because transit damage claims require courier insurance verification, please record a short continuous video while unboxing your wooden crate. If any damage or mirror crack is found, send the video and clear photos to WhatsApp at +91 93281 43036 or email support@evaraart.in within 48 hours of delivery.

Upon verification, we will immediately initiate your choice of:

  • An immediate complimentary hand-painted replacement created and dispatched with priority express tracking.
  • OR a 100% full refund credited back to your original payment method.

2. 7-Day Return Window (Ready-to-Ship Artworks)

We want you to adore how your artwork illuminates your room. If a standard catalog artwork does not harmonize with your interior space:

  • You may request a return within 7 calendar days from the date of confirmed delivery.
  • The artwork must be in its original, pristine condition, unmounted, and safely re-packed in its protective cushioning and hardboard crate.
  • We will arrange a reverse pickup through our insured courier partners. A nominal reverse logistics shipping fee (₹250–₹350 depending on size) is deducted from the refund amount.

3. Bespoke Custom Commissions Policy

Custom commissions are uniquely personalized according to your room dimensions, spiritual deities, and color swatches. Lead artist Shraddha Parmar shares high-resolution work-in-progress photos and videos on WhatsApp for your direct review and approval prior to varnishing and dispatch.

Therefore, once approved and dispatched, bespoke custom commission artworks are non-returnable, except in the event of documented transit damage covered by our Zero-Risk Guarantee above.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

Once your returned artwork arrives back at our Gujarat studio and passes physical condition inspection:

  • Refunds are approved and initiated within 24 to 48 hours of studio receipt.
  • The funds are credited back to your original source of payment (UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, NetBanking via Razorpay) within 3 to 5 business working days as per banking network standards.
  • You will receive an automated confirmation email and SMS once the refund is completed.
